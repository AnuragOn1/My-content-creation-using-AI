# Series plan: Days 03–07 (drafts)

These are draft hooks and lesson beats so the reels can be batch-produced. Each is about 85 words of voiceover, around 30s. I'll tighten them once the Day 01/02 numbers are in (hook retention decides the first line, average watch time decides the length).

| Day | Hook (0–3s) | The skill | Proof (before → after) | Tease |
|-----|-------------|-----------|------------------------|-------|
| 03 | "Stop answering AI. Make it ask YOU." | End every big prompt with: *"Before you start, ask me 5 questions you need answered to do this well."* | A generic study plan → one built around "2 hrs/day, weak at graphs, interview in 6 weeks" | Day 04: prompt chaining |
| 04 | "One giant prompt is why your output is mid." | Prompt chaining: **Outline → Draft → Critique → Fix**, one step per message | A one-shot README → a chained README with a setup section, an example and an FAQ | Day 05: learn DSA faster |
| 05 | "Use AI as a DSA tutor, not a cheat sheet." | *"Don't give me the code. Give me one hint at a time, then quiz me on the pattern."* + ask for the brute force first, then the optimal solution | Copy-pasted solution → explaining Two Sum's hashmap idea in your own words | Day 06: build a project in 1 hour |
| 06 | "I built this in one hour with AI." | Spec first (*"Ask me questions, then write a 10-line spec"*) → scaffold → one feature per prompt → AI code review | "Make me an app" chaos → a working React to-do with filters + deploy | Day 07: interview prep |
| 07 | "Let AI interview you before Flipkart does." | *"You're a senior interviewer at [company]. Ask me one question at a time, score my answer out of 10, then give me a better version."* | A rambling STAR answer → a tight 60-second answer with numbers | Week 2 kicks off |

**Recurring structure:** hook → DAY XX title → what most people do → the fix in 3 animated steps → before/after → SAVE THIS → next-up + follow button.
**Recruiter angle:** Days 05–07 put your own engineering path on screen (DSA, a shipped project, interview prep). Those three are the most useful as proof of skill.
