# The 5 prompts (DM this to everyone who comments PROMPTS)

Copy everything below the line into the DM.

---

Here are the 5 prompts I use with Claude every day. Swap the [brackets] for your own details.

**1. Plan my day**
> Here's my study plan: [paste plan]. Today is [date]. Turn today into a checklist split into my study blocks ([e.g. 12:30–2:30 DSA, 2:30–3:30 course, 3:30–6 project]). Keep it realistic for [N] hours, and flag anything I should push to tomorrow.

**2. Hints, not answers**
> I'm solving [problem name]. Here's my attempt: [paste code]. Don't give me the solution or any code. Give me one hint, the smallest nudge that gets me unstuck. If I ask again, make the next hint a little bigger.

**3. Explain it with my own project**
> I'm building [your project, e.g. a real-time chat app with React, Node and Socket.io]. Explain [concept, e.g. async/await] using an example from my project. Keep it under 150 words, then ask me one question to check I understood.

**4. Senior-dev code review**
> Review this code like a senior developer reviewing a pull request. List what you would reject and why, most important first. Don't rewrite everything. Only show the lines that need to change.
> [paste code]

**5. The 2 AM check-in**
> Here's today's to-do list with what I finished: [paste list]. Tell me exactly what's still open, and the one or two things I should finish before I sleep. Be blunt.

Tip: if you use Claude with Notion connected, you can ask it to read your to-do page directly instead of pasting it.
