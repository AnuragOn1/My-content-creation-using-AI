Stop answering AI. Make it ask YOU. 👇

One vague prompt = AI fills the gaps with guesses.
Fix it with one line:

1️⃣ ASK FIRST: add "Before you start, ask me 5 questions you need answered."
2️⃣ ANSWER: give real details (time, deadline, level, tools, goal)
3️⃣ THEN WRITE: let it build the plan around YOUR answers

Example: "make me a DSA study plan" went from a generic week-by-week list to a 6-week, 2 hrs/day Python plan that starts graphs from zero (my weak spot).
