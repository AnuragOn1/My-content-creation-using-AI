You're using AI wrong. Here's the 4-part prompt that fixes it 👇

Every good prompt has 4 parts:

1️⃣ ROLE: who the AI should be ("You are a senior tech recruiter.")
2️⃣ TASK: exactly what to make ("Write a cover letter for a Frontend Developer role at Flipkart.")
3️⃣ CONTEXT: what it needs to know ("I have 2 years in sales and built 3 React projects.")
4️⃣ FORMAT: length, structure, tone ("Under 150 words, 3 short paragraphs, confident tone.")

Template: You are a [ROLE]. [TASK]. Context: [CONTEXT]. Format: [FORMAT].
