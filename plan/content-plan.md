# Content plan

**Goal:** an Instagram page about using AI to its full capacity, tied to the engineering career push. Finished, upload-ready reels so the only job left is posting.

## Weekly rhythm

| Slot | Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trend video | Reach | Motion graphics with a new model · "I tried [new AI tool] so you don't have to" · "Same prompt, two models" |
| Value video | Saves and follows | "5 things I use Claude for every day" |
| Comment-bait video | Comments and DMs | "Comment TUTORIAL and I'll show how" · "Raw prompt vs final result" · "Rate my first attempt 1–10" · "Which AI tool should I test next?" |
| Day series | Habit and return viewers | "Day 1, Day 2, Day 3…" journey teaching AI, each episode teasing the next |

Standalone reels (like the two in `reels/`) sit outside the Day series and don't tease a next day.

## Hook patterns (from 4 reference reels)

Reuse the structure, never anyone's exact wording or video.

1. **Borrow a big moment.** Ride a big brand's or model's launch, add your own version, credit your tools.
2. **"N things to do with [tool] before [next step]."** Numbered list, with lines that handle objections ("it doesn't do the work for me, it makes sure I do it").
3. **Result first.** Show the finished thing, let viewers ask for the tutorial, then make it. *(Portfolio reel → TUTORIAL comments → tutorial reel.)*
4. **"First attempt."** Raw vs edited, before/after. Beginner framing performs well.

## Backlog

- **Tutorial: how the portfolio reel was made.** Make it once TUTORIAL comments arrive (pattern 3).
- **Same prompt, two models.** Trend slot.
- **Raw prompt vs final result.** Comment bait; the portfolio reel's prompt and loading screen are ready-made raw material.
- **Which AI tool should I test next?** Comment bait; the answers become trend videos.

## Tools

- **Visuals:** this repo's engine (HTML → MP4). Canva is connected for one-off graphics.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs (male voice Utkarsh). The free tier is disabled on the account, so decide on a paid plan, another tool, or recording your own voice.
- **Video generation:** Higgsfield free tier, still untested.

## Monetization (context only)

Indirect income first (freelance work, referrals, interview calls), then affiliate links, brand deals, and your own products once the audience trusts you. Expect months of posting before the first income.
